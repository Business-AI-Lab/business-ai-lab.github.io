**Dr. Thien Van Luong** is a lecturer at the Faculty of Data Science and Artificial Intelligence, National Economics University (NEU), and the founder and head of Business AI Lab. He received his PhD from Queen's University Belfast in 2019 and was a Research Fellow at the University of Southampton (2019-2021).

## Research Interests

- Deep Learning & Neural Networks
- Computer Vision & Image Processing  
- Natural Language Processing
- Wireless Communications
- Internet of Things (IoT)
- AI for Business & Finance

## Key Achievements

- **50+ peer-reviewed publications** in leading venues including IEEE Transactions, Journal of Lightwave Technology, and major AI conferences
- **Golden Globe Award for Science & Technology 2022** - Awarded by the Vietnamese government for outstanding young scientists
- **AI City Challenge Champion 2024 & 2025** - NVIDIA's premier computer vision competition for city-scale video understanding
- **PhD Queen's University Belfast (2019)** with expertise in wireless communications and signal processing
- **Research Fellow University of Southampton (2019-2021)** - Postdoctoral research in advanced communications systems

## Education

- **PhD in Electronics and Electrical Engineering**, Queen's University Belfast, UK (2019)
- **MSc in Electronics and Telecommunications**, Hanoi University of Science and Technology
- **BEng in Electronics and Telecommunications**, Hanoi University of Science and Technology
