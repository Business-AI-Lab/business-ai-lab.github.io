BUSINESS AI LAB

Dr. Thien Van Luong · Founder & Lab Leader

Connecting foundational AI with real-world impact.

Dr. Thien Van Luong is a lecturer at the Faculty of Data Science and Artificial Intelligence, National Economics University, and the founder of Business AI Lab. His research spans applied AI for business and finance, computer vision, NLP, IoT and advanced wireless communications, with over 50 peer-reviewed publications in leading venues including IEEE Transactions and Journal of Lightwave Technology.
